Assembly

The PCB (92 × 99.5mm) fits both Hammond 1455 enclosure variants, held by a 3D-printed internal frame. PCB and HDD sit side-by-side, SATA connector direct-mates between them.

Cutouts Required

  • Barrel jack or screw terminal (12V DC input)
  • RJ45 (Ethernet)
  • USB-C
  • Tactile button (recessed)
  • RGB LED (light pipe or translucent window)
