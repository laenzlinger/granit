## Enclosure Options

The PCB (92 × 99.5mm) fits both Hammond 1455 enclosure variants, held by a
3D-printed internal frame. PCB and HDD sit side-by-side, SATA connector
direct-mates between them.

### Slim — Hammond 1455L2201

220 × 103 × 30.5 mm, 2.5" HDD only.

![Slim assembly](assembly-slim.png)

### Wide — Hammond 1455T2601

260 × 160 × 51.5 mm, 2.5" or 3.5" HDD.

![Wide assembly](assembly-wide.png)

## Cutouts Required

- Barrel jack or screw terminal (12V DC input)
- RJ45 (Ethernet)
- USB-C
- Tactile button (recessed)
- RGB LED (light pipe or translucent window)
